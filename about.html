About KrazyBuy

KrazyBuy is an AI-powered price intelligence platform that helps shoppers compare products across India's leading online stores in seconds. Powered by Retzo AI, our goal is to make online shopping smarter, faster, and more transparent.

Our Mission

Our mission is to simplify online shopping by bringing prices from multiple trusted retailers into one place. Instead of opening many shopping apps, users can search once on KrazyBuy and quickly compare available prices to make informed buying decisions.

Powered by Retzo AI

Retzo AI analyzes product information from supported retailers, compares available listings, and highlights the best available options. It is designed to provide fast, clear, and easy-to-understand shopping insights while keeping the experience simple for users.
